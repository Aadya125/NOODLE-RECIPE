NOODLES

Noodles are staple foods, often seasoned with spices and vegetables which add a burst of flavor
Use red chillis, onions, and butter to make them extra delicious!

Ingredients required

  • Noodles (whole wheat)
  • Red chillis
  • Onions
  • Butter
  • salt
  • tomato puree

Instructions

  1. boil the noodles in slated water
  2. heat a pan and add butter and pinch of spices
  3. add onions and tomato puree and let in simmer
  4. add the noodles and saute them till cooked
  5. serve it hot with a hint on spices and butter on top
  6. enjoy!!
